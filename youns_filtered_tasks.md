# Youns's Task Roadmap: Cycle Weeks 5 to 8 (Simplified Layouts)

This document presents a structured, prioritized, and filtered list of your tasks as **Youns** for Weeks 5 to 8. It has been fully updated to reflect the simplified layout rules from your boss (no eggs, no blocks, and characters/pets standing next to each other).

## 📋 Role Overview & Simplified Layout Rules
You are responsible for the **2D visual identity** and **game assets** for both **Shared Anime** events and **RTTO (Original)** updates. Your deliverables for each week include:
1. **Game Thumbnail (GTHB)** (High Priority)
2. **Event Thumbnail (ETHB)** (High Priority)
3. **Currency Icon (CURI)** (High Priority, formerly Ranu's role)

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Simplified Design Rules**:
> - 🚫 **NO EGGS OR BLOCKS**: Thumbnail layouts must **NOT** contain 3D Egg models or 3D Block models anymore. Ignore them in composition plans.
> - 🧍 **SIDE-BY-SIDE LAYOUT**: The thumbnails should simply display the spotlight characters (for Shared Anime) or the weekly pets (for RTTO) standing side-by-side next to each other.
> - 🤖 **ROBLOX FORM & INTERNET REFERENCES**: The spotlight characters must be in **Roblox form** (as requested by Zach). You do **NOT** need the "VIRUS" reference file; you can instruct your AI image generators to make the characters based on their names/info available on the internet.
> - 🐳 **RTTO PETS DEPENDENCY**: RTT: Original is the *only* game that has its own unique weekly pets. You will coordinate with **Zero** to obtain these 4 3D pet models and compose them side-by-side.
> - 🪙 **CURRENCY ICON (CURI)**: Stands alone as a 2D themed asset, independent of 3D models.
